# IT vs OT — OpticWise Stance & 30-Second Explainer

**Status:** Canonical stance — `Canon — IT vs OT Stance` (priority 1)
**Source:** Bill Douglas, Slack message to Danny / OWnet v1 punch list, May 11, 2026
**Apply as:** Active stance/policy on every relevant response (not passive lookup)

---

## Analogy (use as opener)

- **IT = the brains of the company** (email, PM systems, accounting).
- **OT = the nerves and muscles of the building** (what actually moves air, water, people, and bits).

---

## 1. Definitions

### IT — Information Technology

Runs the business.

Examples:
- Email, office apps
- Yardi / RealPage / MRI
- CRM, accounting, BI tools

### OT — Operational Technology

Runs the building.

Examples:
- BMS/BAS & HVAC controls
- Lighting control systems
- Access control, parking, gates
- Cameras / NVRs
- Submeters, leak detection, sensors & gateways
- On-site switches, firewalls, controllers, edge servers

---

## 2. OpticWise POV (for messaging)

- Most owners **have** an IT strategy.
- Almost none **have** an OT strategy — vendors and integrators quietly own it.
- OpticWise helps owners:
  - Take back control of OT and the data it generates.
  - Connect IT + OT under an **Owner Data Standard (ODS)**.
  - Turn digital infrastructure (especially OT) into a **long-term value engine**, not a sunk cost.

### Core line (use on calls)

> "You've got a plan for IT. We help you build a plan for OT — the tech that actually runs your buildings and drives NOI."

---

## 3. Quick Discovery Questions

Use these to open the IT vs OT conversation:

1. "Who really owns and controls your building systems — your team, or your vendors?"
2. "If you wanted to move your BMS / access / leak detection data into a new tool tomorrow, how hard would that be?"
3. "Do you have the same level of strategy and governance for OT that you do for IT?"

---

## 4. Brand Tie-In (close the loop)

> "We sit at the intersection of IT and OT.
> Our job is to help you own your digital infrastructure,
> make your OT and data actually perform,
> and build for the long game."

---

## Stance enforcement (for OWnet agent)

When IT, OT, or any OT-adjacent term (BMS, BAS, HVAC controls, access control, submeters, leak detection, cameras, building network, edge controllers) appears in a user prompt or response:

1. **Frame OT-first.** Lead with the OT gap, not IT. Owners feel the OT pain; IT is usually handled.
2. **Always pair them.** "IT + OT under an Owner Data Standard" — never treat OT in isolation.
3. **Avoid the trap.** Do not describe OpticWise as an "IT company" or an "OT vendor." We're the layer that unifies them under owner control.
4. **Use the analogy when explaining to non-technical owners.** Brains vs nerves/muscles is the canonical opener.
5. **Drop the core line** in any sales-context response or discovery-prep ask.
