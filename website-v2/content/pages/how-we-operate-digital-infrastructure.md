# How OpticWise Operates Data & Digital Infrastructure

Most owners have an IT strategy. Almost nobody has an OT strategy.

That’s where control gets lost—quietly—through low-voltage decisions, vendor silos, and dashboards that trap your data.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

## What We Mean by “Operate”

OpticWise provides managed services that include **design, implementation, and operation** across facilities and portfolios—keeping performance high and operational risk low **without burdening on-site teams**.

Operating means you’re not “done” at install.

You have an owner-controlled foundation that stays reliable, governed, documented, and adaptable as vendors and needs change.

---

## Layer 1: Managed data & digital infrastructure

### Design (standardize once)
We design repeatable standards across properties so your foundation doesn’t become a one-off every time:
- Network and wireless design aligned to building use
- Coverage and capacity planning
- Segmentation and access rules vendors must follow
- Documentation standards (so accountability is real)

### Implementation (go-live without chaos)
Your low-voltage contractor installs wiring/equipment to OpticWise specifications. OpticWise configures the in-building network and brings it live in coordination with your go-live schedule.

Retrofits can be implemented in less than one quarter; new construction stays synchronized with GC schedules.

### Operations (keep performance high)
After go-live, we operate the foundation continuously:
- Monitoring and performance management
- Maintenance coordination and lifecycle hygiene
- Support orchestration across vendors and carriers
- Documentation upkeep so knowledge doesn’t disappear when teams change
- Governance enforcement (segmentation, access, and “no shadow networks”)

**Outcome:** performance stays high, risk stays low, and your on-site team is not turned into the digital help desk.

---

## Layer 2: Owner-controlled intelligence layer (OpticWise Brain)

Once the foundation is standardized and governable, we enable the owner-controlled intelligence layer:

A vendor- and LLM-agnostic **Property Intelligence Layer** with a governed **data plane + trust plane** that makes each property capable of autonomous activities and intelligence.

Plain language:
- The **data plane** organizes and carries usable data
- The **trust plane** governs identity, access, privacy, lineage, retention, and rules of use

**Outcome:** you can plug in any decision engines you want (vendor platform, internal analytics, any LLM)—and swap them over time—because the foundation remains yours.
