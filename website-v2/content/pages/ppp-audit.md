Peak Property Performance® (PPP™) is OpticWise’s owner-first operating model for turning data & digital infrastructure into measurable outcomes—NOI growth, tenant experience outcomes, operational control, and future-proofing / AI readiness. It's also a best-selling book published by Fast Company Press.

PPP Audit™ is the Clarify entry point: it maps what you own, where value is leaking, and what data is trustworthy and portable—so you can standardize once and scale.

It’s how you stop buying point solutions that trap your data inside a single building—and start building an intelligence layer that scales.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

---

## What the PPP Audit™ Does

The PPP Audit™ establishes a clear understanding of:
- **What you own** (and what you don’t)
- **Where value is leaking** (NOI, OpEx, vendor friction, redundancy)
- **Who is accountable** for integration outcomes (not just installs)
- **What data is trustworthy and portable**
- **Where you have the least control** (vendors, networks, integrations, data access, maintenance workflows)

This is not a “tech assessment.”  
It’s an ownership and operating model reset.
