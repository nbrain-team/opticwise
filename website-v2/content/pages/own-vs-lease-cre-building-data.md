# Own vs Lease CRE Building Data

Owning CRE building data means the property owner retains full control, access, and decision rights over operational and tenant-generated data, while leasing data places those rights in the hands of vendors and platforms.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

---

## Why This Distinction Matters Now

For years, “smart building” decisions were framed as feature decisions.

But data ownership is an **economic control** decision:
- Who can export the data
- Who holds admin credentials
- Who defines the “truth” about performance
- Who can change platforms without rewiring the building

Across a portfolio, leased data becomes structural disadvantage.

---

## What It Looks Like to Lease Your Data

You’re leasing your data when:
- Data is accessible only through a vendor dashboard
- Exports are limited, priced, or technically blocked
- Admin credentials are held by a third party
- Integrations require custom work every time
- Your building’s “intelligence” is someone else’s asset

The result: every new tool becomes another silo, and every property becomes a one-off.

---

## What It Looks Like to Own Your Data

You own your data when you have:
- **Export rights** and practical access (not “available upon request”)
- **Admin credentials** under owner control
- Governed identity and permissions (who can see what, and why)
- Repeatable standards across properties (so intelligence scales)
- Portability so platforms can be swapped over time

Owning data is not a dashboard. It’s a durable capability.

## FAQs

**FAQ: What does it mean to “own” building data?**  
Owning CRE building data means the property owner retains full control, access, and decision rights over operational and tenant-generated data—supported by export rights, admin credentials, and portability.

**FAQ: What’s the risk of leasing data?**  
Leasing data puts control in vendor hands. Over time that creates lock-in, limits your ability to benchmark across a portfolio, and turns “intelligence” into someone else’s asset.

**FAQ: What does “vendor- and LLM-agnostic—forever” mean?**  
It means you can run any decision engines you want (vendor platform, internal analytics, any LLM) under owner permissions—and you can change those engines over time because the foundation stays yours.
