# BoT® (Building of Things®)

Building of Things® (BoT®) is OpticWise’s strategic approach to consolidate all your property’s networks onto the fewest possible physical networks—**reducing build cost and operating cost while increasing control**.

It strengthens the foundation you own: your **data & digital infrastructure**.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

---

## The Problem BoT® Solves

Most buildings don’t have an “IoT problem.”  
They have a **control problem**:

- Networks get built in silos (one per vendor, one per system, one per “solution”)  
- Low-voltage and OT decisions get locked early without owner governance  
- Data ends up fragmented and trapped inside dashboards  
- Every new tool becomes another one-off integration

That’s how you end up designing a building… and accidentally designing vendor lock-in.

---

## What BoT® Changes

BoT® makes the building operate like a coordinated system instead of a collection of vendor deployments.

### Consolidate
Fewer physical networks. Less redundant equipment. Lower CapEx and OpEx.

### Standardize
Repeatable standards across properties so you don’t recreate silos building by building.

### Govern
Segmentation, access rules, documentation, and accountability—baked in.

### Scale Intelligence
A stable foundation you own so Property Intelligence becomes Portfolio Intelligence.
