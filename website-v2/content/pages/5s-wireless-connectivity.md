# 5S® Wireless Connectivity

5S® is OpticWise’s wireless connectivity product. It delivers the **5S® user experience (UX)** over owner-controlled **data & digital infrastructure**—so connectivity becomes a controllable asset, not a vendor dependency.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

---

## What 5S® Delivers

5S® is defined by five non-negotiable outcomes:

- **Seamless Mobility** — reliable roaming across the property without “dead zones” or session drops  
- **Security** — governed access and segmentation so users and systems don’t share the same risk surface  
- **Stability (resilience)** — engineered reliability to reduce interruptions and operational escalations  
- **Speed** — performance designed for real tenant/resident behavior and building demands  
- **Service** — a support experience that doesn’t turn your property team into the help desk
