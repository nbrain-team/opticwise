# Data & Digital Infrastructure NOI Playbook

A data & digital infrastructure NOI playbook is a repeatable, owner-led framework that turns commercial real estate data & digital infrastructure, systems, and data into **predictable NOI** instead of unmanaged operating costs.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

## Why a Playbook (Not a One-Off Project)

Most “smart building” efforts fail for a simple reason: they’re treated like implementations, not operating models.

A playbook makes NOI performance **repeatable**:
- Across assets
- Across vendors
- Across renovations and refresh cycles
- Across portfolio acquisitions and transitions

Don’t play for next quarter—build for the next decade.

## The PPP 5C™ Owner Path

### 1) Clarify (PPP Review/Audit)
Establish a clear understanding of what you own, where value is leaking, and how disconnected your data & systems really are.

What you walk away with:
- Ownership map (admin credentials, data rights, exports, network control)
- Where operational burden stacks up vs your KPIs
- What data is trustworthy and portable
- Accountability: who owns integration outcomes (not just installs)

### 2) Connect
Create a resilient digital backbone that links all building systems, operational platforms, and devices.

What “good” looks like:
- No shadow networks
- Segmented access rules vendors must follow
- Documented network and OT governance

### 3) Collect
Aggregate high-fidelity, usable data from across your property.

What “usable” means:
- Consistent naming and metadata
- Reliable ingestion and retention
- Exportable under owner permissions (not trapped in dashboards)

### 4) Coordinate
Use data to optimize operations, align vendors, and automate processes across your building(s).

What changes:
- Fewer manual escalations and fewer “mystery” failures
- Standard workflows that reduce cycle time
- Vendor alignment to owner rules and outcomes

### 5) Control
Reclaim ownership of your data & digital infrastructure, vendor ecosystem, and OT tech stack.

Control means:
- You can swap platforms without rewiring buildings
- Intelligence compounds across the portfolio
- Outcomes are driven by your standards—not vendor lock-in
