# CRE AI Readiness

CRE AI readiness is the condition in which a commercial real estate owner controls their **data & digital infrastructure**, data access, and system integration well enough for AI to produce reliable, actionable insights — **without vendor lock-in, data loss, or black-box dependencies**.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

---

## What “AI readiness” really is

AI readiness is not “we bought an AI tool.”

AI readiness means you have the owner-controlled foundation that makes AI outputs **trustworthy, governed, and repeatable**:
- Identity and permissions (who can access what, and why)
- Data access and export rights (not trapped inside dashboards)
- Integration accountability (so systems don’t become silos)
- Documentation and standards (so performance doesn’t degrade over time)
- Portability (so intelligence compounds across buildings)

AI readiness is a **data & digital infrastructure ownership decision**.

---

## Why AI fails in most buildings

AI fails when the inputs are fragmented and ungoverned:
- Multiple vendor silos and shadow networks
- “Smart” platforms that can’t export clean data
- Missing context (metadata, naming standards, lineage)
- No clear rules for privacy, retention, and permitted use

AI can’t fix a foundation you don’t control.

## FAQs

**FAQ: Is AI readiness about buying AI tools?**  
No. AI readiness is a data & digital infrastructure ownership decision. Tools come and go; the foundation must remain yours.

**FAQ: What is the OpticWise Brain?**  
A vendor- and LLM-agnostic Property Intelligence Layer: a governed data plane + trust plane that makes each property capable of autonomous activities and intelligence.

**FAQ: What do “data plane” and “trust plane” mean in plain language?**  
They’re the governed foundation that makes building data usable and controlled: the data plane organizes/carries usable data; the trust plane governs identity, access, privacy, lineage, retention, and rules of use.
