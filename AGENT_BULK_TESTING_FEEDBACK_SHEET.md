## Evaluation Criteria

For each response, evaluate on a scale of 1-5:

### 1. Brand Voice Adherence (1-5)
- ✅ Uses "you" language (owner/operator POV)
- ✅ Positions OpticWise as trusted guide (not vendor)
- ✅ Uses "digital infrastructure" (not just "infrastructure")
- ✅ Includes reframing line or similar strategic positioning
- ✅ Avoids PropTech jargon / sounds like trusted advisor

### 2. Content Quality (1-5)
- ✅ Provides specific, actionable information
- ✅ Ties features to outcomes (NOI, control, retention, etc.)
- ✅ Uses PPP 5C framework appropriately
- ✅ Addresses the actual question asked
- ✅ Provides appropriate depth (not too shallow, not overwhelming)

### 3. Formatting & Scannability (1-5)
- ✅ NO emoji icons (clean, professional text only)
- ✅ Uses headers, bullets, and structure
- ✅ Easy to skim and understand
- ✅ Sources appear in collapsible dropdowns (not expanded)
- ✅ Proper use of bold, lists, and spacing

### 4. Objection Handling (1-5)
- ✅ Addresses potential concerns proactively
- ✅ Validates concerns before reframing
- ✅ Provides reassurance with specifics
- ✅ Doesn't sound defensive or salesy
- ✅ Builds trust through transparency

### 5. Call to Action (1-5)
- ✅ Includes appropriate next step
- ✅ Mentions PPP Audit or similar entry point
- ✅ Feels natural (not forced)
- ✅ Aligned with infinite game framing
- ✅ Easy for prospect to take action
