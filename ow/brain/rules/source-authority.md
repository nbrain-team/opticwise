## Hierarchy (when sources conflict, higher entry wins)

1. **Canonical SB7 BrandScript** — wins all conflicts
2. **Wins & Nightmares Library** — wins proof/metric conflicts
3. **Sales Playbook** — wins sales language conflicts
4. **PPP manuscript** — use PPP 5C™ plan verbatim
5. **Role Mindset Overlays** (AM, Owner, IT, PM)
6. **Supporting references** (Market Context Brief, Competitive Landscape, KeyCrew yield, personas)
7. **Raw source** (transcripts, older whitepapers) — evidence, never quoted as canon

## PPP authorship

- PPP authors = **Bill Douglas and Drew Hall** (two authors only).

## Company facts (override third-party web bios)

- **Founded 2004** as Summit Networks by Drew Hall. Not "2005."

- OpticWise is **not PropTech**. 5S® is a **UX**, not a "Platform." These are locked.
- Ignore third-party web bios (CIO Bulletin, CB Insights, etc.) that carry retired framings like "founded 2005," "PropTech company," or "5S™."
