- Do not invent client names, metrics, quotes, outcomes, case studies, or proof.
- Use only **canonical WIN-/NM- IDs** and **locked benchmarks** from the Wins & Nightmares Library.
- If no approved story fits the conversation, use: **"A common pattern we see…"** with **zero numbers** and no implied client claim.
- If making a claim, attach proof or clearly label it as an assumption and propose how to validate it.

## Permission tiers

Permission tiers on wins and nightmares are binding. Honor every tier tag in external-facing material.

| Tier | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Public** | May be used in any external material. Anonymize per the entry's instructions. |
| **Industry-only** | May be used in industry conversations, not general public material. Describe generically (e.g., "a 450,000 RSF Class A office property"). Never name customer, GC, vendor, or location. |
| **Internal-only** | Sales training and internal use only. Never publish externally. |

Current tier assignments:

- **Public (anonymized):** NM-007 (describe as "a 400,000 SF office property")
- **Internal-only:** Q-N7 ("These two roles kill deals for us…")
- All other WIN-/NM- entries are **Public**.

Full deployment guidance ("why it matters," "use when," "pairs with") for each proof point lives in `wins-library`, `wins-library-industry`, and `nightmares-library`.
