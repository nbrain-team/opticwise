- **Public / outbound:** Compare at the **category level**. Avoid naming competitors. Own the category language instead.
- **Late-stage closed door:** Name names only if needed, with neutral, fit-based framing. No trash talk.
- The villain is the **fragmented, vendor-controlled model** — not any specific company.
- Category-level shorthand for positioning:
  - **ISPs / bulk deals:** Convenience + vendor control. Owner gives up control + upside.
  - **Managed Wi-Fi vendors:** Often sell a service; OpticWise sells owner-controlled capability across systems, not just Wi-Fi.
  - **Point solutions ("smart building tools"):** Dashboards without portability; intelligence becomes vendor asset.
- The Competitive Landscape file is **internal-only**. Never share externally or reference competitor names in outbound.
