## Mandated terms

- Always say **"data & digital infrastructure"** — never "infrastructure" alone. Never abbreviate to **"DDI"** and never reverse the word order to "Digital & Data Infrastructure."
- **"owner-controlled"** — the default modifier for what OpticWise builds. Export rights, admin credentials, portability, and governance written into contracts.
- **"vendor-agnostic"** — OpticWise's design philosophy. No vendor lock-in; vendors plug in under owner rules.
- **"AI-ready"** — the state an asset reaches when its data & digital infrastructure is owner-controlled, governed, and portable. Not a feature; a property of the foundation.
- **"data-generating assets"** — how to frame buildings that have owner-controlled data & digital infrastructure. Infrastructure is not background plumbing; it is strategic control, future optionality, performance leverage, and a monetizable asset.
- Always **"owner"** instead of "REIT."
- Always **"operations"** or **"data"** instead of "ESG." Do not reference ESG in OW content or LinkedIn. Refer instead to **utilities optimization**.
- Always **"5S® UX"** — never "5S® Platform." 5S® is a user experience, not a platform.
- In sales conversation and outbound, say **"review"** not "audit." (PPP Audit™ stays as formal trademark language in marketing/contracts.)
- **"digital backbone"** is allowed only when it clearly refers to data & digital infrastructure.

## Mandated reframe (use often)

> "If you don't own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do."

## Banned terms

- Never label OpticWise as **"PropTech."** We are a strategic advisor + operator layer.
- Never use **"DDI"** or **"Digital & Data Infrastructure"** — canon is "data & digital infrastructure" (data first, no acronym).
- Never **"5S® Platform"** — canon is "5S® UX."
- Never **"infrastructure"** alone — always "data & digital infrastructure."
- Never **"REIT"** — always "owner."
- Never **"ESG"** in OW content or LinkedIn.

## Definitions (quick reference)

- **CRE** means commercial real estate.
- **Peak Property Performance®** is a CRE strategy book focused on data and digital infrastructure — not a tech or AI book.
- **SIC®** — Security, Infrastructure, Connectivity. Both a design strategy and a certified platform: the hardened, secure, infrastructure-connected platform that Layer 1 services are designed to be. SIC® is the result / the deliverable — not merely a standard or framework. Components within the SIC® platform include BoT®, CaaS, and 5S® UX.
- **BoT® (Building of Things®)** — the standard. OpticWise's owner-controlled backbone encompassing all sensors and connected "things." OW's term used instead of "IoT." BoT® is a component within the SIC® platform.
- **Connectivity-as-a-Service (CaaS)** — the connectivity component within the SIC® platform. CaaS is never the layer, never the platform, never OW's headline — it is one piece of the managed-infrastructure foundation. **ElasticISP®** is an optional piece within CaaS. Never present CaaS as freestanding or as equivalent to Layer 1; never let it position OW as a connectivity/Wi-Fi provider. Hierarchy: owner-controlled intelligence layer (Property Brain™, the product) → managed data & digital infrastructure (Layer 1) → SIC® (the platform) → CaaS (connectivity component within SIC®) → ElasticISP® (optional within CaaS).
- If a sentence needs acronyms or buzzwords to sound smart, rewrite it in plain English with a concrete example.
