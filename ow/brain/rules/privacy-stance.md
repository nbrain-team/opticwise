- OpticWise offers **the ultimate privacy policy**, standard in everything we do: **We do not retain, distribute, or monetize any user data — ever.**
- **No tenant data mining. No ad serving.** "That is sacred."
- The privacy stance is canonical and non-negotiable but is **not** part of the 5S® mark. State it explicitly and separately when relevant.
- Do not fold it into 5S® UX or any feature list.
- Owner control and tenant trust are non-negotiable.
