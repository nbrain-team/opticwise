- **Peak Property Performance® (PPP)** — OpticWise's CRE strategy book and operating framework, focused on data and digital infrastructure (not a tech or AI book).
- **PPP 5C™ plan** — Clarify · Connect · Collect · Coordinate · Control. The path from review to portfolio-scale intelligence.
- **PPP Review / Audit** — The entry-point engagement. Mapped, scoped, paid. Surfaces ownership, leakage, and the practical path to control. In sales conversation, say "review."
- **SIC®** — Security, Infrastructure, Connectivity. Both a design strategy and a certified platform: the hardened, secure, infrastructure-connected platform that Layer 1 services are designed to be. SIC® is the result — the deliverable — not merely a standard or framework. Owner-controlled. Vendor- and ISP-agnostic. First-tier equipment only. Resilient by design. Components within the SIC® platform: BoT®, CaaS, 5S® UX. Full architecture: see `two-layer-model`.
- **BoT® (Building of Things®)** — The standard. OpticWise's owner-controlled backbone encompassing all sensors and connected "things" in a building. OW's term used instead of "IoT." A component within the SIC® platform. Full architecture: see `two-layer-model`.
- **ElasticISP®** — An optional piece within CaaS (Connectivity-as-a-Service). Owner-controlled, ISP-agnostic managed connectivity with dual-circuit redundancy at the MPOE. Full architecture: see `two-layer-model`.
- **5S® user experience** — Seamless Mobility · Security · Stability (resilience) · Speed · Service. Five tenant outcomes the OW network delivers. This is a UX, not a "platform."
- **Property Brain™ → Portfolio Brain™** — Vendor- and LLM-agnostic intelligence layer. Standardize at one property; scale to portfolio. Plug in any decision engine, swap any vendor, without losing data or governance.
- **Owner-controlled by design** — Export rights, admin credentials, portability, and governance written into contracts.

## Naming clarifier

*Conceptually:* Property Intelligence → Portfolio Intelligence. *Productized:* Property Brain™ → Portfolio Brain™.
