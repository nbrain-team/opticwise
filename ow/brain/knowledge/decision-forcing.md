## Why deals stall

Deals stall when we over-explain, accept deflection, and act like a vendor. The shift: stop explaining solutions, start framing decisions.

## Core shift

**Old role:** Vendor explaining solutions.
**New role:** Strategic advisor framing decisions.

"I don't sell technology. I help owners decide whether they want their asset to perform."

"I am not here to agree. I am here to clarify reality and force a decision."

## 3-step pattern

1. **Acknowledge** — "That makes sense..."
2. **Reframe** — "...but that assumes this is a cost center..."
3. **Force decision** — "So which are you optimizing for — cost or asset performance?"

## The CapEx reframe (cost center → income-producing asset)

Use when the blocker is CapEx resistance or inertia around existing infrastructure.

**Winning frame:** Old = cost center. New = income-producing asset.

> "You're not deciding whether to spend capex. You're deciding whether to convert a weak system into an income-producing asset."

**Key scripts (use verbatim):**

> "Right now, your property's technology is a cost center. We turn it into an income-producing asset that drives NOI and increases value."

> "So the decision isn't whether to spend capex — it's whether you want this asset performing or just functioning."

> "Which side are you optimizing for — cost center or asset?"

## Deflection killer: "I'll ask my team"

"That makes sense — you want your team aligned. But when this gets pushed down, it usually defaults to no change."

"Before it goes there, I need to understand — if this drives NOI and asset value, is this something you want to pursue?"

If YES:

"Great — then let's align on that first, and bring your team in to implement, not decide."

## Behavioral rules

- If you catch yourself explaining, stop and restate as a decision.
- When you feel hesitation around positioning, default to the stronger, clearer, more direct version immediately.

## Sales philosophy

- Fast NO > Slow maybe
- Frame > Explain
- Decision > Discussion
- Asset owner thinking > Vendor thinking

## Live deal checklist

**Before any meeting:**
- What decision am I forcing?
- What frame am I establishing?
- What deflection will I counter?

**During meeting:**
- Am I explaining or asserting?
- Did I force a decision?

**After meeting:**
- Is there a clear YES or NO?
- Or did it drift?

## One-slide weapon (required for stalled deals)

Must include: NOI impact, IRR / payback, property value increase. Numbers > explanation.
