## First-call structure (30 minutes)

1. **Open with the Big Three framing question:** "Three plays drive most of the NOI lift in CRE optimization — utilities, insurance, occupancy. Which are you most accountable for, and where do you have the least operating visibility right now?"
2. Outcomes + accountability (NOI / utilities / insurance / retention / hold horizon)
3. Control posture (who owns what; how many networks; contracts; MPOE)
4. Operational burden (tickets, outages, vendor sprawl, PM bandwidth)
5. The "one-property" path (review → pilot → scale)
6. Close with decision: schedule review or disqualify fast

**Close lines (pick one):**

- "If you can't answer who owns the network, the review writes itself. Want to map it in one building and decide with facts?"
- "The data to play these is already being generated in your buildings. The question is whether you own it or your vendors do."

## The "writes itself" qualifier questions

- "How many networks are running on the property?" ('I have no clue' is the qualifier.)
- "Who owns the equipment in your MPOE — you or the ISP?"
- "Do you have an active contract on file? Do you know its exit terms?"
- "What's your hold horizon on this asset?"
- "Who's the property manager, and would they be willing to walk us through the closets?"
- "Do you have architectural plans or network diagrams from acquisition?"

If they can't answer the first three, the review writes itself.

## Mandatory discovery questions (outcomes + constraints)

- "What outcomes are you accountable for: retention/avg lease term, OpEx/O&M, utilities, insurance, lease-up, NOI, exit multiple?"
- "Where do you have the least control: vendors, networks, integrations, data access, maintenance workflows?"
- "Third-party PM? What's non-negotiable for them — and what won't they change?"
- "When do low-voltage/OT decisions lock (SD/DD/GMP/submittals/turnover)? Who owns integration accountability?"
- "What asset types dominate the next 12–24 months?"
- "If you had monthly 'plays' (not dashboards), what 3 decisions would you improve?"

## 4 constraints to test early

1. GC/Sub inertia (spec drift, VE games, rushed turnover)
2. Third-party PM constraints (standard tools/workflows, resist one-offs)
3. Data skepticism ("we don't have a BI team") → translate into actionable plays, not dashboards
4. Asset economics (garden/walk-up ≠ 5-over-1; don't default to managed Wi-Fi assumptions)

## The Triangle Gap (why owners are flying blind)

The structural reason owners lose control without feeling "judged":

- **Property manager:** tenant-facing (leasing, service, retention)
- **Asset manager:** financial-facing (NOI, capex, reporting)
- **IT manager:** employee-facing IT (not the property's operational stack)

The triangle in the middle — OT + tenant internet + building controls + access + video + HVAC data — is nobody's job. That's where OpticWise focuses.
