- **Legal Name:** OpticWise, Inc.
- **Structure:** Delaware Corporation
- **Headquarters:** Golden, Colorado
- **Founded:** 2004 (originally Summit Networks) by Drew Hall
- **Rebranded:** 2016
- **CEO:** Bill Douglas
- **Founder & Chief Architect:** Drew Hall

## Positioning (canonical)

OpticWise helps CRE owners and operators turn data & digital infrastructure into owner-controlled digital assets — so Property Intelligence becomes Portfolio Intelligence. OpticWise is NOT PropTech.

## Primary offers

- PPP Review / digital infrastructure review (the Clarify entry point)
- One-building pilot (prove portability and governance)
- BoT® architecture
- ElasticISP® managed connectivity
- 5S® UX tenant experience

## GTM focus

- Multifamily, multi-tenant office, mixed use, hospitality
- Typical sales cycle: 3+ months
- Delivery model: OW executes unless explicitly told otherwise
