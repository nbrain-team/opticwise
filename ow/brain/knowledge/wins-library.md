## WIN-001 — From Shelfware to Savings (Office Property) | Public

In one office property, an existing lighting control system had been installed but was never properly operationalized. OpticWise audited it, turned it on, and put it into active operation. The property saved approximately $70,000 in annual utilities — year over year — while becoming more occupied during the same period. Same building. Same revenue. Less spend. The system was already paid for.

- **Why it matters:** Proves the difference between buying tech and owning data & digital infrastructure as an operating asset.
- **PPP 5C:** Clarify, Coordinate, Control
- **Use when:** Owner says "we already bought tools," "we already have dashboards," or "ROI isn't clear."
- **Pairs with:** WIN-006 (Voltage Spike — same principle, different data type: shelfware data → operationalized utility savings vs. voltage data → operationalized capital protection).

## WIN-002 — Portfolio Moves From NOI Results to NOI Drivers | Public

Instead of comparing only NOI/unit or NOI/sf, owners compare causal drivers (utilities variance, ticket velocity, equipment failure patterns, water risk visibility). Better CapEx planning, fewer emergencies, stronger vendor leverage, compounding performance.

- **PPP 5C:** Collect, Coordinate, Control
- **Use when:** Owner wants portfolio benchmarking or proactive CapEx planning

## WIN-003 — Sub-Metering the Mixed-Use Building | Public

A mixed-use property had a 24/7 call center, a 9-to-5 dental office, and other tenants on different operating clocks — all sharing utility costs split evenly under a triple-net allocation. OpticWise installed sub-meters and surfaced actual consumption per tenant. Billing was rebuilt to reflect actual usage.

The owner now bills accurately, has documentation behind every charge, and the cross-subsidy is gone. The dentist isn't subsidizing the call center's overnight runtime, and the call center has no objection — they're the ones using the power. A recurring tenant-relations friction point quietly disappeared.

- **Why it matters:** A play that's nearly impossible without owner-accessible operating data, and entirely possible with it. Demonstrates how owning the data plane converts what looks like a billing dispute into a defensible, documented allocation.
- **PPP 5C:** Connect, Collect, Coordinate
- **Use when:** Owner asks for a tangible example, owns mixed-use or multi-tenant office, or is splitting utilities by SF rather than actual use.
- **Pairs with:** Big Three Plays diagnostic (Utilities lever).

## WIN-004 — One-Year Payback on a 299-Unit Multifamily | Public

A 299-unit Class A multifamily property (built 2020, traded 2023) deployed the full OpticWise two-stack NOI model. On the income stack, 5S® WiFi and ancillary tenant-facing services produced approximately $624 per door per year. On the expense stack, utilities optimization added $36, insurance improvements added $22, and reduced OT OpEx added $12 — totaling $70 per door from the Big Three Plays. Blended annual NOI increase: $694 per door, or $207,506 across the property. Total infrastructure investment: $221,858, or roughly $742 per door. Capitalized at a 4.25% market cap rate, that's $16,329 of asset value per door — approximately $4.88M of asset value created on a single property. Investment payback: about one year. Conservative equity multiple: 20x or better. No rent increases. No market timing.

- **Why it matters:** Proves the two-stack NOI model end-to-end on a single property — both the income stack (5S® WiFi + ancillary) and the expense stack (Big Three Plays) producing capitalized asset value in the same operating cycle. The income-stack contribution dominates the per-door blend, which is why the result lands above the Section F multifamily benchmark range — the Section F range describes Big Three Plays alone; WIN-004 describes the full two-stack outcome. See `noi-benchmarks-and-two-stack`.
- **PPP 5C:** Clarify, Connect, Collect, Coordinate, Control
- **Use when:** Owner asks for hard per-door economics, owner wants payback or equity-multiple math, owner is multifamily, AM or sponsor is anchoring on capitalized value at refi or exit.
- **Pairs with:** Two-Stack NOI Model (`noi-benchmarks-and-two-stack`), WIN-002 (NOI Results → NOI Drivers), NM-006 (Diligence Discount).
