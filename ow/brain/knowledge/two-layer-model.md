## Layer 1 — Managed Data & Digital Infrastructure (the owner-owned foundation)

Managed data & digital infrastructure services: design, implementation, and operation across CRE facilities and portfolios — keeping performance high and operational risk low without burdening on-site teams.

Layer 1 delivers standalone value. Data can be managed at Layer 1 on its own, without requiring Layer 2 (Property Brain™ / Portfolio Brain™). This is why the layers are separable — an owner can start with Layer 1 and add Layer 2 when ready.

### SIC® — the platform

**SIC®** stands for Security, Infrastructure, Connectivity — describing what the platform IS, not an abstract framework. SIC® is both a design strategy and a certified platform: the hardened, secure, infrastructure-connected platform that Layer 1 services are designed to be. Owner-controlled. Vendor- and ISP-agnostic. First-tier equipment only. Resilient by design. SIC® is the result — the deliverable — not merely a standard.

### Components within the SIC® platform

- **BoT® (Building of Things®)** — the standard. OpticWise's owner-controlled backbone encompassing all sensors and connected "things" in a building. BoT® is OW's term used instead of "IoT." BoT® consolidates and governs building connectivity so every device or system can run on a single, secure, segmented foundation.

- **CaaS (Connectivity-as-a-Service)** — the connectivity component within the SIC® platform. CaaS is never the layer, never the platform, never OW's headline — it is one piece of the managed-infrastructure foundation. **ElasticISP®** is an optional piece within CaaS: owner-controlled, ISP-agnostic managed connectivity with dual-circuit redundancy at the MPOE.

- **5S® UX** — the user-experience layer across Layer 1: Seamless Mobility · Security · Stability (resilience) · Speed · Service. Privacy is one of the five S's. 5S® is a UX, not a platform.

### Composability

SIC®, BoT®, and secure infrastructure are composable, not strictly dependent. A prospect can have secure infrastructure without the full SIC® platform, or without BoT® (e.g., if they don't want OW gathering all device data). This is both an architecture fact and a sales fact — it lets the engagement start where the owner is ready.

## Layer 2 — Owner-Controlled Intelligence Layer (Property Brain™ → Portfolio Brain™)

A vendor- and LLM-agnostic Property Intelligence Layer: a governed data plane + trust plane that makes each property capable of autonomous activities and intelligence. Standardize it once, and Property Brain™ becomes Portfolio Brain™ — so intelligence compounds across buildings instead of restarting at every address.

## The strategic point

Most competitors operate in a single tier. OpticWise operates the two tiers that own and govern everything else. Decision engines (any LLM, any vendor app, internal analytics, autonomous workflows) plug in on top — swappable. Building systems, OT, point apps, and vendors plug in underneath — under owner rules.

## Outcome

One standard intelligence substrate, many decision engines, scaled across many buildings.
