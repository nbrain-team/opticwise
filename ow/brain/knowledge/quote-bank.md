## Canonical quotes

- "We're asking way too much from property managers relative to technology."
- "Duplicate networks and redundant systems are wasted CapEx and wasted OpEx."
- "If you own your data & digital infrastructure, switching vendors is not that difficult."
- "If you only look at NOI, you're looking at the result — not the cause."
- "Technology should be treated like an income-generating amenity: an asset, not an expense."

## Added April 2026 (KeyCrew interview)

- **Q-N1:** "Digital is the equal asset to your countertops, your facades, your carpet, your glass, your signage. If you're showing it as an expense, your strategy is off."
  - *Use context: Reframes digital from cost line to asset class. Strong board-deck and CFO line.*

- **Q-N2:** "It's way beyond being smart. It's autonomous."
  - *Use context: Forward-pivot from "smart building" cliché to OW's actual value (governed, action-taking infrastructure).*

- **Q-N3:** "Asset managers are financial quants. They can run forecasts. They don't know the levers driving the numbers."
  - *Use context: Use to depersonalize the "asset manager doesn't know" pattern. Honest, not insulting.*

- **Q-N4:** "Why don't you have a digital architect and a digital engineering firm? You have one for the building. Why not for the data & digital infrastructure?"
  - *Use context: Use early in owner conversations. Shifts the ask from "buy a tool" to "build a function."*

- **Q-N5:** "If you're not monetizing your data, somebody else already is."
  - *Use context: Direct, urgent. Pair with: "It should offend you. It's your building, your tenants, your data."*

- **Q-N6 (refined):** "Status quo is the biggest barrier to entry. It works — until it doesn't."
  - **As spoken:** "Status quo is our biggest barrier to entry. It works. Leave it alone." (then refute)
  - *Use context: Honest acknowledgment of customer inertia, used to disarm the "we're fine" objection. (Then refute.)*

## Insert-anywhere nugget reframes

- "Most owners have an IT strategy. Almost nobody has an OT strategy."
- "Owners shouldn't be forced to run portfolios with blurry vision."
- "Portability changes the power dynamic: vendors compete to serve you, not trap you."
- "If it's free, you're the product. Your vendors are using your building, your tenants, and your data. That should offend you."
- "Most owners hire an architect for the building. Almost none hire one for the data & digital infrastructure."

- "Everyone is nominally responsible and nobody is actually accountable."
- "Status quo bias is the primary barrier. The buildings work, owners are busy doing deals, and the data sits unused for another year."
- "The data exists. The owner legally owns it. They just can't access it."
