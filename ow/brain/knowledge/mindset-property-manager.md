Use when a property manager is in the room. This is a **conversion playbook** — the PM is not the buyer.

## Why the PM matters

The PM doesn't sign and doesn't pay. But a PM who doesn't want this can slow-walk it into the ground. The PM kills implementations, not contracts — which is worse than losing a deal cleanly.

## PM psychology

- **Time-poor to the point of triage.** Every decision filtered through "will this create work for me?"
- **Fears more support calls above almost everything.** New tech = new tickets they field for free.
- **Fears disruption and tenant backlash.** A messy transition means angry tenants, bad reviews, furious owner — all landing on PM.
- **Wants to look good upstairs.** Graded on retention, reviews, occupancy, "no surprises."
- **Protective of status quo because change has burned them.** NM-003 taught caution. Honor it.
- **All blame for building tech, none of the tools, none of the budget, none of the time.**

## Converting the PM

| Blocker | Response |
|---|---|
| "This means more support calls." | "We take 100% of connectivity support, 24/7. You never see those calls." |
| "Transitioning residents will be arduous." | "No rip-and-replace. Capture new leases first. You pick the cadence." |
| "Residents will complain / I'll get blamed." | "5S® means it works. When anyone has a question, they call us, not you." |
| "We don't have bandwidth." | "You don't take it on. We operate it. Bandwidth back, not bandwidth spent." |
| Silent slow-walk | Surface caution with cover. "You've lived through systems that never worked right. We own it, you set the pace." |

## PM language patterns

Use: off your plate, we take the calls, no rip-and-replace, you pick the cadence, fewer fires, fewer complaints, retention, reviews, lease-up velocity, amenity, white-label.

Do NOT use with PM: SIC®, VLANs, MPOE, ElasticISP® — translate these into plain outcomes. "VLAN isolation" → "every tenant private and secure on their own lane." "Dual-ISP failover" → "backup takes over, tenant never notices."

## The villain (PM frame)

> "When the building's network is a patchwork of vendor contracts, every outage becomes your phone call and your one-star review — for a service you don't even bill."

## The win to hand the PM

Fewer tickets, zero connectivity support load, happier tenants, better reviews, cleaner lease-up story, easier reporting, cadence they control, zero added headcount — and a result they can take credit for upstairs.

## What NOT to do

- Do not pitch PM as the buyer (not their budget or decision)
- Do not lead with NOI, cap rate, DSCR, or exit math — that glazes PMs
- Do not imply they've been doing it wrong — it's a structural unfairness
- Do not let support-call fear go unaddressed — pre-empt it
- Do not propose a big-bang rollout
- Do not bypass them — a steamrolled PM lets implementation rot
