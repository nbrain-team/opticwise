## Core sales motion (simple, repeatable)

### Default next step (non-pitch)

Offer a 30-minute introductory call to understand the property, constraints, and upside. Then propose a PPP Digital Infrastructure Review (paid).

### Crawl / Walk / Run (canonical closer)

"Crawl, walk, run. Let's focus on one property. After you decide whether to kill it or move forward, then we'll talk about the others."

### Free-first-review motion (warm intros / trusted partner path)

For warm intros from trusted partners:

- Free first review on one property (normally $5,000)
- Bill attends with one engineer if the property warrants
- One day on site — afternoon arrive, morning walk
- If the engagement moves forward, the $5K is applied to services
- Output: written summary of what's there, what's recommended, and CapEx/NOI implications
- Explicit framing: "I want to see the other systems that are there, not just the Wi-Fi."
- Resist scope creep: "Let's focus on one property."

## Managed Wi-Fi / wired internet product economics (isolated)

These are isolated product economics — separate from the locked canonical NOI benchmarks (Big Three Plays compounded effect), which live in `noi-benchmarks-and-two-stack`.

| Asset type | Play | Typical owner NOI lift |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-rise / high-rise multifamily | Managed Wi-Fi (full distribution) | $40–$50 per door per month |
| Garden-style multifamily | Managed wired internet (still NOI-impacting) | $25–$35 per door per month |
| Bulk revenue share (status quo) | — | 4–6% of ISP billing |
| Owner-operated managed Wi-Fi | — | ~50% margin on tenant billing |

**Do not** combine these with the locked NOI benchmarks (Big Three Plays). They model different things.

### Density rule of thumb for Wi-Fi planning

- Dense buildings: ~1,800 sq ft per access point
- Garden-style: ~900 sq ft per access point (more APs → more CapEx → harder to justify; wired internet is usually the play)

## 5S® user experience (UX) — quick reference for sales

Five tenant outcomes the OW network delivers:

1. **Seamless Mobility** — works everywhere on the property
2. **Security** — VLAN isolation per tenant
3. **Stability (resilience)** — dual-ISP failover, on-site firewall
4. **Speed** — distributed network, not last-mile-throttled
5. **Service** — 24/7 support included

Privacy stance is canonical but is *not* part of the 5S® mark. State it explicitly alongside 5S when relevant — see `rules/privacy-stance`.
