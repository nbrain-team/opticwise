These are responses that have landed on real calls. Use verbatim or adapt lightly to context.

| Objection | Response that landed |
|---|---|
| "Property managers won't want the support calls." | 24/7 support included. We handle every tenant onboarding call (Sonos, smart bulbs, "grandma forgot her password," all of it). The PM does not see those calls. |
| "We only own this 24–36 more months." | NOI lift goes to bottom-line at sale. The faster you start, the more NOI you can show on the offering memo. (Pairs with NM-006.) |
| "Won't transitioning existing residents be arduous?" | Capture new leases first. Roll-everyone-at-once is possible but not required. The PM picks the cadence. |
| "Garden-style won't justify Wi-Fi CapEx." | Agreed — wired internet is the play. Still nets $25–$35/door/month, well above what a rev share returns. |
| "We assumed the contract at acquisition and don't know what we have." | That's exactly what the review surfaces. We tell you what's there, who owns it, and what your options are. No commitment past the review. |
| "ISP just emailed offering us a bulk deal — should we take it?" | They emailed because they know they can pay you 6%. If they can pay you 6%, you can earn far more by distributing the network yourself and capturing ~50% margin on billing. |
| "We already have dashboards." | Dashboards aren't durable capability. The issue is ownership + portability + governance. |
| "This will be disruptive." | No rip-and-replace. Start with Clarify, then phase around turnover and CapEx timing. |
| "Our vendors already handle it." | If you don't own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do. Portability changes the power dynamic. |
| "ROI isn't clear." | Tie to preventable failure modes + specific monthly plays (utilities variance, ticket velocity, after-hours emergencies, water risk, renewals). Or run the Big Three Plays diagnostic. |
| "We don't have bandwidth." | We operate the owner standard without taxing PMs/on-site teams. Different skill sets. (Pair with Right Butt, Wrong Seat.) |
| "We tried AI / data analytics already and it didn't work." | You can't apply machine learning to data you turned on yesterday. You need six to nine months of usable history before patterns emerge. PPP Clarify + Collect builds that history under owner control. |
| "Why should we move now? The buildings work." | Status quo bias is the primary barrier in this industry — and it has a cost. When you trade or refi, the acquirer's diligence will find recoverable NOI you weren't capturing. (NM-006.) |
| "We're not technologists." | You shouldn't have to be — but you are responsible for asset performance. So do you want this driving NOI, or just sitting there as a cost? |

## Technical proof points (simple, owner-grade)

Use these when the conversation moves into "how does this actually work":

- Tenant gets their own VLAN — works in their unit, by the pool, in common areas, in a friend's unit. Secure from every other tenant.
- Two ISPs into the MPOE = automatic failover. Tenant doesn't have to know which one is active.
- White-label: owner names it (e.g., "MyFi," "[Property]Net," "technology amenity package").
- Wi-Fi 6 vs Wi-Fi 5 is rarely a tenant decision. Wired can; air often can't.
- Add-ons: dedicated IP for gamers (e.g., $25/month, 50/50 split with owner) and other custom services priced similarly.
- Equipment is sourced from first-tier manufacturers (HP, Cisco, Schneider, Fortinet). No white-labeled or off-brand equipment.
