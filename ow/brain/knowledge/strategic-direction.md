## Strategic identity

OpticWise is pursuing **category leadership in owner-controlled CRE data & digital infrastructure.** The strategy rests on four pillars:

1. **Consultative sales motion.** Sell owner control, not internet. Lead with PPP positioning, the Big Three Plays diagnostic, and the two-layer model. Typical cycle: 3+ months. Entry point: PPP Review on one property.

2. **PPP as category-defining IP.** Peak Property Performance® (the book, podcast, and framework) creates sales pull and positions OpticWise as the authority — not a vendor.

3. **Recurring revenue at portfolio scale.** Move from single-property engagements to owner standards that compound across portfolios.

4. **The OWnet brain as the knowledge backbone.** Consolidate all canonical knowledge into a single store so every tool (Cursor, Claude, OWnet) reads the same source of truth. Reduce drift. Scale the brain as the company scales.

## GTM focus

Multifamily, multi-tenant office, mixed use, hospitality. Office-to-resi conversions are an emerging greenfield opportunity.
