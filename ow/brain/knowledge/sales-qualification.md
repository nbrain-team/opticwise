## Bullseye ICP (at a glance)

- Multifamily owner-operator, Class A / luxury, 10–30 deals
- Institutional capital partners (NY Life, AIG, large pension/insurance LPs)
- Third-party PM (Greystar, Asset Living, Gallery Residential, Bozzuto)
- 24–60 month hold horizon
- Mix of garden-style + mid-rise
- Currently receiving reactive ISP outreach they can't evaluate

## Disqualifiers

- In-house IT controlling the network plane (owner isn't actually seeking owner-control)
- Sub-Class B (economics/tenant price sensitivity often breaks)
- Sub-18-month flippers
- Single-asset owners (cost of sales + cost of implementation mismatch)

## Fit gates (internal)

- Owner-sponsor wants control (not "set it and forget it")
- Willing to standardize: one owner standard, vendors plug in
- Will start with Clarify (no skipping to tools/dashboards)
- Will run a real pilot and prove portability
- Will enforce governance so we can operate the standard
