BoT® (Building of Things®)

The connective layer for owner-controlled digital infrastructure

From the Internet of Things to the Building of Things

Most owners are familiar with the Internet of Things — sensors, controllers, devices, and systems generating signals across a property every second.

The problem is not that buildings lack connected things. The problem is that those things are operated by vendors — not by the owner.

BoT® (Building of Things®) reframes this reality. If you own a building full of digital systems and devices, you should operate it as your own Building of Things — not as a collection of vendor-controlled IoT deployments.

Connected Building Systems

What BoT® Does

BoT® is the connective layer that turns networks, sensors, systems, data environments, and AI into one owner-controlled digital nervous system.

Unifies Systems

Connects disparate networks, sensors, and devices into a coordinated whole

Preserves Data

Ensures data continuity and ownership regardless of vendor changes

Compounds Intelligence

Enables AI and analytics to learn and improve over time

Owner Control

Places you, not vendors, in control of your digital infrastructure

Frequently Asked Questions

How is BoT® different from IoT?

IoT describes connected devices. BoT® describes owner-controlled operation of all digital things within a building as a unified system.
