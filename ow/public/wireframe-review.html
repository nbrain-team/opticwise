Data & Digital Infrastructure NOI Strategy

Data & digital infrastructure NOI strategy is the intentional design, ownership, and control of a property’s networks, systems, and data to directly increase net operating income, reduce operational risk, and protect long-term asset value.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.
Why NOI Is Now a D&DI Decision

Why NOI Is Now a Data & Digital Infrastructure Decision

For years, owners treated networks and building systems like background utilities. But today, the foundation determines: Who controls connectivity economics, Whether operations are manual or coordinated, Whether data is usable or trapped, Whether new tools add capability or add silos.

When the foundation is vendor-controlled, NOI becomes fragile and inconsistent. When the foundation is owner-controlled, NOI becomes repeatable.

Where NOI Leaks

Where NOI Leaks Today

Most leakage isn’t obvious on day one. It shows up quietly over time:

  • Revenue-share connectivity agreements that cap upside and limit flexibility
  • Redundant infrastructure when each vendor builds their own network
  • Data trapped in dashboards that can’t be exported or governed
  • Manual operations because systems don’t coordinate
  • Integration fragility that creates downtime, truck rolls, and escalations
  • Portfolio inconsistency where each building becomes a one-off

Each item feels manageable. Together, they erode NOI year after year.

Owner-Control Advantage

The Owner-Control Advantage

  • Higher effective revenue through owner-controlled connectivity and amenity economics
  • Lower OpEx through standardization, coordination, and fewer failure points
  • Reduced operational risk through governed access, documentation, and accountability
  • Portfolio compounding because standards repeat and intelligence scales

You’re not upgrading tech. You’re upgrading your business model.

Own vs Lease CRE Building Data

Owning CRE building data means the property owner retains full control, access, and decision rights over operational and tenant-generated data, while leasing data places those rights in the hands of vendors and platforms.

Sections

Why This Distinction Matters Now

Data ownership is an economic control decision: Who can export the data, Who holds admin credentials, Who defines the “truth” about performance, Who can change platforms without rewiring the building. Across a portfolio, leased data becomes structural disadvantage.

What It Looks Like to Lease Your Data

Data accessible only through vendor dashboard, Exports limited/priced/blocked, Admin credentials held by third party, Integrations require custom work every time, Building’s “intelligence” is someone else’s asset.

What It Looks Like to Own Your Data

Export rights, Admin credentials, Governed identity and permissions, Repeatable standards, Portability. Owning data is not a dashboard. It’s a durable capability.
