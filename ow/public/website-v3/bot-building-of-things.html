BoT® (Building of Things®)

Strategic approach to consolidate all your property’s networks onto the fewest possible physical networks—reducing build cost and operating cost while increasing control.

The Challenge

The Problem BoT® Solves

Most buildings don’t have an IoT problem. They have a control problem.

01

Networks Built in Silos

Every system—access control, HVAC, lighting, parking, AV—arrives with its own network, its own switch, its own VLAN, its own vendor portal. Nobody planned how they’d coexist.

02

Low-Voltage / OT Decisions Made Without Governance

The integrator picks the hardware, the vendor picks the cloud, and the owner never sees the decision until it’s installed and invoiced.

03

Data Fragmented Across Vendor Dashboards

Building data exists in dozens of places you can’t export from, can’t normalize, and can’t query across properties.

That’s how you end up designing a building… and accidentally designing vendor lock-in.

The Shift

What BoT® Changes

Consolidate

Fewer networks, lower CapEx and OpEx. One backbone instead of a dozen vendor-owned overlays.

Standardize

Repeatable standards across properties. Build once, deploy everywhere, compound outcomes.

Govern

Segmentation, access rules, documentation, accountability. Know who touches what—and why.

Scale Intelligence

Move from Property Intelligence to Portfolio Intelligence. Every building feeds the same data plane.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Building of Things® (BoT®)?
BoT® consolidates all of a property’s networks onto the fewest possible physical networks. Instead of every system bringing its own switch, its own VLAN, and its own vendor portal, BoT® creates a single governed backbone. It often saves hundreds of thousands in build costs—and the operating savings compound year over year.
Are we designing a building… or designing vendor lock-in?
That’s the question BoT® forces you to answer before it’s too late. Consolidate first, then every system added follows owner standards—not vendor defaults. The result: you can swap vendors, upgrade systems, or sell the asset without ripping out the foundation.
