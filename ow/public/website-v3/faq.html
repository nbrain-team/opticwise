FAQ: Owning Your Data & Digital Infrastructure

Real questions from CRE professionals who are making the shift from vendor convenience to owner control.

Layer 1 — Managed Data & Digital Infrastructure
How do we avoid designing vendor lock-in?
Specify owner-controlled pathways from day one—admin credentials, export rights, and open-standard cabling. If your spec doesn’t include these, you’re designing a building someone else will own operationally.
What should our low-voltage spec include?
At minimum: owner-owned fiber backbone, dedicated riser space, conduit separation between vendor and owner pathways, and documented admin access for every connected system.
How does BoT® reduce build costs?
BoT® consolidates vendor infrastructure onto a shared owner-controlled backbone, reducing redundant cabling, MDF/IDF buildouts, and ongoing maintenance contracts.
Layer 2 — Owner-Controlled Intelligence
What is the OpticWise Brain?
It’s the intelligence layer that sits on top of your managed infrastructure—aggregating data across systems so you can make portfolio-level decisions, not just building-level ones.
Can we plug in our own AI tools later?
Yes. That’s the point. When you own the data plane and the trust plane, you can swap AI engines without re-wiring your building or re-negotiating vendor contracts.

When should we engage OpticWise?
Ideally during pre-construction or design development—before low-voltage specs are locked. The earlier we engage, the more cost you avoid and the more control you retain.
Layer 1 — Managed Data & Digital Infrastructure
How do I know if vendors own my infrastructure?
Ask three questions: Who holds admin credentials? Can you export clean data? Can you swap the platform without rewiring? If you can’t answer all three, your vendors likely own more than you think.
What does managed D&DI include?
Managed Data & Digital Infrastructure covers connectivity backbone, access control pathways, network segmentation, credential governance, and ongoing operations—all under owner control.
Will this replace our existing systems?
Not necessarily. The goal is to establish owner control over the foundation so your existing systems connect to something you own, rather than something a vendor controls.
Layer 2 — Owner-Controlled Intelligence
What is Property Intelligence → Portfolio Intelligence?
Property Intelligence gives you building-level visibility. Portfolio Intelligence aggregates that across assets so you can benchmark, standardize, and compound value at scale.
What does vendor-agnostic mean?
It means your intelligence layer isn’t locked to any single vendor’s dashboard. You own the data plane, so you choose which tools consume it—and you can change them without disruption.
How does this affect asset value?
Owner-controlled infrastructure reduces diligence risk, strengthens the investor narrative, and creates a repeatable operating standard that compounds across the portfolio.

The What & Why
What does OpticWise actually do?
OpticWise provides owner-controlled data & digital infrastructure for commercial real estate—Layer 1 (managed infrastructure) and Layer 2 (intelligence layer)—so owners retain control, reduce vendor dependency, and compound value across portfolios.
Why does ownership of data & digital infrastructure matter?
Because whoever owns your infrastructure owns your operational data, your tenant experience, and your ability to make AI-ready decisions. If you don’t own it, your vendors do.

Definitions
What is ElasticISP®?
ElasticISP® is OpticWise’s connectivity delivery model—carrier-neutral, scalable bandwidth provisioned over the owner-controlled backbone. It replaces fragmented ISP agreements with a single governed pathway.
What is BoT®?
BoT® (Building of Things) is OpticWise’s converged infrastructure platform that consolidates building systems—access control, Wi-Fi, IoT, BAS, cellular—onto a single owner-controlled backbone.
What is PPP™?
PPP™ (Peak Property Performance) is OpticWise’s owner-first operating model for turning data & digital infrastructure into measurable outcomes—NOI growth, tenant experience, operational control, and AI readiness.
