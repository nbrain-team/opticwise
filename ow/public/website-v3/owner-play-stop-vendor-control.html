Owner Play: Stop Letting Vendors Own Your Data & Digital Infrastructure

A simple monthly play to reclaim control without adding burden to your on-site team.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

Most CRE owners don’t realize how much operational control they’ve handed over—not through bad decisions, but through default ones. Every vendor contract that doesn’t specify admin credentials, export rights, or platform portability is a decision to let someone else own your building’s digital backbone.

This isn’t theoretical. It shows up in your NOI, your tenant experience, your diligence narrative, and your ability to scale a repeatable operating standard across a portfolio.

The Three Questions That Reveal Who Controls Your Building

Before running any play, answer these three questions for every connected system in your building:

  1. Who holds admin credentials? If you can’t log in as the owner and change settings, the vendor controls the system—regardless of who paid for it.
  2. Can you export clean data? If your data lives inside a vendor dashboard with no export path, you don’t own it. You’re renting access to your own information.
  3. Can you swap the platform without rewiring? If changing vendors means re-cabling the building, you’re locked in. That’s not a technology decision—it’s an ownership failure.

If you can’t confidently answer all three, your vendors own more than you think.

The Monthly Play: Admin Credentials Review

This is the simplest, highest-impact play you can run. One building. One hour. Once a month.

Step 1: Inventory

List every connected system in the building—access control, HVAC/BAS, Wi-Fi, cellular, elevator, parking, visitor management, tenant portals. For each system, document: vendor name, platform, and who holds the admin login.

Step 2: Reclaim

For any system where the vendor holds the only admin credentials, request owner-level access. If they can’t or won’t provide it, document that as a control gap.

Step 3: Document

Create a simple ownership map: system, vendor, admin access (owner/vendor/shared), export capability (yes/no), platform portability (yes/no). This becomes your baseline.

Step 4: Govern

Make this review a recurring operational cadence. Every new vendor, every new system, every renewal—the same three questions. No exceptions.

Why This Matters for NOI

Owner control of data & digital infrastructure isn’t a technology initiative. It’s an NOI strategy.

  • Revenue leakage: Fragmented ISP agreements, unaudited revenue shares, and vendor-controlled billing create invisible losses.
  • Operating cost: Redundant vendor contracts, duplicate infrastructure, and manual workarounds inflate OpEx.
  • Risk exposure: Shadow networks, ungoverned access, and non-portable data weaken your compliance and insurance posture.
  • Diligence weakness: Buyers and lenders want to see a governed, repeatable operating standard—not a patchwork of vendor relationships.

Where to Start

Pick one building. Run the admin credentials review. See what you find. If the answers surprise you, that’s exactly why this play exists.
