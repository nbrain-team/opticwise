PPP Audit™

Peak Property Performance® (PPP™) is OpticWise’s owner-first operating model for turning data & digital infrastructure into measurable outcomes—NOI growth, tenant experience outcomes, operational control, and future-proofing / AI readiness. It’s also a best-selling book published by Fast Company Press.

PPP Audit™ is the Clarify entry point: it maps what you own, where value is leaking, and what data is trustworthy and portable—so you can standardize once and scale.

It’s how you stop buying point solutions that trap your data inside a single building—and start building an intelligence layer that scales.

If you don’t own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do.

What It Does

What the PPP Audit™ Does

The PPP Audit™ establishes a clear understanding of your data & digital infrastructure landscape.

What you own

(and what you don’t)

Where value is leaking

NOI, OpEx, vendor friction, redundancy

Who is accountable

For integration outcomes (not just installs)

What data is trustworthy

And portable

Where you have the least control

Vendors, networks, integrations, data access, maintenance workflows

This is not a “tech assessment.” It’s an ownership and operating model reset.

Deliverables

What You Walk Away With

Ownership + Control Map

  • Admin credentials and access ownership
  • Export rights and portability status
  • Vendor boundaries and responsibilities
  • Shadow network detection and remediation plan

  • Insurance and risk narrative

03

A Repeatable Standard (Not a One-Off Fix)

A recommended owner standard you can productize:

  • The rules vendors must follow
  • The foundation you can scale across properties
  • The path from Property Intelligence → Portfolio Intelligence
