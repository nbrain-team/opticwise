Delivery

How 5S® Is Delivered

01

Protocol-Agnostic by Design

5S® is delivered over Wi-Fi, in-building cellular, DAS, or other protocols depending on building requirements. The technology choice follows the use case—not the other way around.

02

Owner Control Is the Point

Regardless of protocol, the defining principle is the same: the owner controls the infrastructure, the data, the standards, and the support model. Connectivity becomes an asset on your balance sheet—not a line item on a vendor’s.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 5S®?
5S® is OpticWise’s wireless connectivity product delivering five pillars of user experience: Seamless Mobility, Security, Stability/Resilience, Speed, and Service—all over owner-controlled data & digital infrastructure.
Will the entire property be covered?
Yes. Coverage and capacity are engineered to building use patterns, not just square footage. That means lobbies, elevators, parking decks, outdoor amenity areas—everywhere people and devices actually operate.
Can tenants make calls deep inside the building?
Yes. Wi-Fi calling and in-building cellular are part of the 5S® design. Coverage extends to stairwells, basements, and interior core areas where traditional cellular often fails.
How many users/devices can it handle?
Capacity is designed for real-world behavior patterns, with headroom for growth. That includes simultaneous video calls, cloud applications, IoT telemetry, and guest access—not theoretical peak throughput numbers.
Who supports tenants?
OpticWise orchestrates support. Your team is not the digital help desk. Issues are triaged, resolved, and documented through OpticWise’s operations model—so your property staff can focus on the building.
