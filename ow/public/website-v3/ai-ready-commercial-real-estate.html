Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does “AI-ready commercial real estate” mean?
Properties with owner-controlled data & digital infrastructure and high-fidelity data that enable predictive operations, resilience, and long-term competitive advantage. It’s not about buying a tool—it’s about building the foundation that makes any tool trustworthy.
Is AI readiness about buying AI tools?
No. Tools come and go; the foundation must remain yours. AI readiness is about governed data, owner-controlled infrastructure, and repeatable standards—so you can plug in any engine and swap it over time without rebuilding.
What does “vendor- and LLM-agnostic—forever” mean?
The intelligence layer is designed so you’re never locked in. Plug in any AI engines, swap them over time, and your foundation—your data, your governance, your standards—stays intact.
What do “data plane” and “trust plane” mean?
The data plane organizes and carries usable data across the property and portfolio. The trust plane governs identity, access, privacy, lineage, retention, and rules—so every data point has context and every action has accountability.
