Own vs Lease CRE Building Data

Owning CRE building data means the property owner retains full control, access, and decision rights over operational and tenant-generated data, while leasing data places those rights in the hands of vendors and platforms.

Context

Why This Distinction Matters Now

Data ownership is not a technical question. It’s an economic control decision.

01

Who Can Export

If you can’t export your own data in a usable format, you don’t own it—regardless of what the contract says.

02

Who Holds Admin Credentials

If the vendor holds the master credentials, they control who sees what and when—and that control shifts the power dynamic.

03

Who Defines “Truth”

If the vendor’s dashboard is your only view, their version of performance becomes the default narrative—for your tenants, your investors, and your team.

04

Who Can Change Platforms

If switching vendors means losing your data history, you’re not making a technology choice. You’re making a lock-in decision.

The Risk

What It Looks Like to Lease Your Data

Dashboard-Only Access

You see what the vendor chooses to show

Exports Limited or Blocked

Your own data behind a paywall or API restriction

Admin Held by Third Party

Vendor controls credentials, access, and permissions

Custom Integration Each Time

No repeatable standard—every project is a one-off

Intelligence Is Someone Else’s Asset

Your building’s data feeds their platform value

The Alternative

What It Looks Like to Own Your Data

Export Rights

Full data export in usable, portable formats at any time

Admin Credentials

Owner holds master access, not the vendor

Governed Identity & Permissions

You define who sees what, when, and why

Repeatable Standards

One framework, every property, every vendor

Portability

Switch platforms without losing your history or intelligence

Owning data is not a dashboard. It’s a durable capability.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to “own” building data?
Full control, access, and decision rights over operational and tenant-generated data. That means export rights, admin credentials, governed permissions, and portability—not just a login to someone else’s dashboard.
What’s the risk of leasing data?
The vendor controls exports, pricing, and the “truth” about your building’s performance. Across a portfolio, this becomes a structural disadvantage—your intelligence, your history, and your narrative belong to someone else.
What does “vendor- and LLM-agnostic—forever” mean?
You’re never locked into any single vendor, AI provider, or model. The foundation is designed for portability and flexibility, so you can change tools, platforms, and engines without losing your data or starting over.
