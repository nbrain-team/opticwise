# Non-Negotiable Content Rules

These rules are enforced both in the prompt and by post-generation validators. Defense in depth.

## Trademark requirements

Every mention of these terms MUST carry its mark on first use in each content piece:
- Peak Property Performance®
- PPP 5C™
- BoT® (Building of Things®)
- ElasticISP®
- 5S®
- SIC®
- Property Brain™
- Portfolio Brain™
- PPP Audit™

## Banned words

NEVER use in any content:
- leverage (in the business-jargon sense)
- synergy
- ecosystem
- holistic
- seamless (except "Seamless Mobility" in the 5S® UX)
- cutting-edge
- PropTech (when describing OpticWise)
- ESG
- DDI (never abbreviate "data & digital infrastructure")
- REIT (always say "owner")

## Mandated language

- Always say "data & digital infrastructure" — never "infrastructure" alone in an OpticWise context.
- Never reverse the word order: "Digital & Data Infrastructure" is wrong.
- Always "owner" instead of "REIT."
- Always "operations" or "data" instead of "ESG."
- Always "5S® UX" — never "5S® Platform."
- In sales-facing content, say "review" not "audit."

## Mandated reframe (must appear)

The following line MUST appear at least once in every blog post and once in every LinkedIn article:
> "If you don't own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do."

## Content format rules

### Blog posts
- 1,500–2,000 words
- Every inline citation must be a live hyperlink
- Every cited source must appear in a "References Cited" footer section
- Open with a sharp observation, data point, or provocative question (NEVER "In today's world" or similar)

### LinkedIn articles
- 1,200–1,800 words
- NO hyperlinks
- NO "References Cited" section
- Conversational, executive voice
- Shorter paragraphs than the blog — written for phone reading
- End with a SPECIFIC question that invites real responses

### LinkedIn short posts
- 150–250 words
- NO hyperlinks
- Hook in line one (no "Excited to share" or "I've been thinking about")
- 3–5 short paragraphs, 1–3 sentences each
- End with hashtags: #OpticWise #CRE #BuildingIntelligence plus 1–2 topic tags

## Audit usage

- "audit" must NOT be used to describe OpticWise's offering or the recommended next step
- Use "review" instead
- Third parties auditing their own systems is fine

## Voice rules

- Grounded. Strategic. Human-forward. Plain language.
- Short punchy sentences. Calm clarity + operator realism.
- Lead with owner outcomes before product details or architecture.
- Default to asset-manager audience lens.
- Don't pitch. Ask. "Conversation, not PowerPoint."
- No corporate bloat, jargon without translation, hype words.
- Rarely use emojis.

## Spoken-voice phrasings (prefer these)

| Concept | Sharpened phrasing |
|---|---|
| Owners need OT strategy | "We're asking IT managers to manage OT. They know firewalls, switches, and servers. They don't know access control from lighting control." |
| Asset managers run blind | "Asset managers are doing optimization in the dark. They're working from reports generated by a property management system that doesn't know how the lighting, elevators, or air conditioners are running." |
| Digital is an asset | "Digital is an investment that generates a return. If it doesn't generate a return, something's wrong." |
| Vendor lock-in | "If it's free, you're the product. Your vendors are using your data. Your building. Your tenants." |
| Role mismatch | "Everyone is nominally responsible and nobody is actually accountable." |
| Visibility gap | "You cannot optimize what you cannot see." |
