# Task: Weekly Intelligence Briefing

Produce the Weekly Intelligence Briefing. Pick the top 5–10 most useful intelligence points from the source list — the ones that move OpticWise positioning, sales, or product thinking.

## Per point

- The insight in 1–2 sentences
- Why it matters to OpticWise (1–2 sentences, name the moat or layer when relevant)
- Source: publication, sender, or organization
- Live hyperlink to the source where available

## Format

- Start with a 2–3 sentence editor's note tying the week's signals together
- End with a "Trends Selected" section naming both trends and one-line rationale each
- Minimum 5 points
- Each point must have a source link where available
